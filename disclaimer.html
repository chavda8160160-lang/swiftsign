Legal Disclaimer

Important Notice Regarding Electronic Signatures & Document Acceptance
Mandatory Disclosure:
SwiftSign creates graphical electronic signatures and business stamps for everyday documents. Acceptance and legal requirements for electronic signatures vary by document type and jurisdiction. SwiftSign does not provide cryptographic digital signatures, certificate-based signing, notarization or legal advice.

1. Graphical Electronic Signatures vs. Cryptographic Digital Signatures

It is essential to understand the distinction between graphical electronic signatures and certificate-based digital signatures:

  • What SwiftSign Creates: Graphical electronic signatures (handwritten strokes drawn on screen or scanned from paper) and business stamp overlays (round seals and rectangular corporate marks) embedded as high-resolution vector objects directly into your PDF document.
  • What SwiftSign Does NOT Provide: PKI (Public Key Infrastructure) digital signature certificates, USB cryptographic e-tokens (Class 3 DSC), hardware security modules (HSM), Aadhaar eSign, Qualified Electronic Signatures (QES under EU eIDAS), or digital notarization.

2. Typical Appropriate Use Cases for SwiftSign

Graphical electronic signatures and digital company seals created with SwiftSign are commonly accepted for commercial and everyday purposes where parties agree to conduct business electronically, such as:

  • Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and mutual confidentiality contracts.
  • Employment offer letters, HR onboarding forms, and employee evaluations.
  • Commercial invoices, purchase orders, delivery challans, and payment receipts.
  • Freelancer service agreements and scope-of-work acknowledgments.
  • School permission slips, internal corporate memos, and office approvals.

3. Documents That Typically Require Cryptographic or Notarized Signing

Certain categories of documents are excluded by law or by institutional policy from simple graphical electronic signatures in various jurisdictions and should NOT rely solely on graphical overlays:

  • Statutory filings with company registrars requiring certified DSC tokens (such as MCA filings in India).
  • Real estate conveyances, land title transfers, and statutory deeds.
  • Wills, codicils, testamentary trusts, and estate planning documents.
  • Court pleadings, affidavits requiring physical oaths before a notary public, or statutory powers of attorney.

4. No Legal Advice

The information provided on this website, inside the SwiftSign application, and in our documentation does not constitute legal advice. If you have questions regarding the enforceability or validity of an electronic signature for your specific contract or jurisdiction, please consult qualified legal counsel.
