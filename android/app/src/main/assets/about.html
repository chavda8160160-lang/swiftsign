About SwiftSign

Fast, local, and private document signing for professionals & small businesses.

Why We Built SwiftSign

Signing a simple one-page NDA, job offer, or invoice shouldn't require creating an online account, uploading confidential company contracts to third-party cloud servers, or paying $20/month for a bloated enterprise signing suite.

SwiftSign was built with a single mission: put complete signing power back in the hands of the user, directly on their own device.

Our Core Architecture Commitments

  • 100% On-Device Local Processing: Documents never leave your phone or computer. There are zero remote cloud servers processing or storing your files.
  • No Account Required: You never need to sign up, log in, or give your email address just to sign a document.
  • True Vector PDF Quality: Unlike basic tools that convert your PDF pages into blurry, low-resolution raster images, SwiftSign injects your signatures and seals directly into the underlying vector PDF streams. Text remains crisp and selectable.
  • Zero Watermarks Forever: Every document exported by SwiftSign—on both Free and Pro plans—is 100% clean, professional, and free of promotional watermarks.
  • Official Company Stamp & Seal Generator: Create 1800px high-resolution transparent PNG round seals and rectangular stamps with 11 standard business templates, ready to use on contracts or export to WhatsApp and Word.

Platform Model

SwiftSign on the web is completely Free forever. For users on Android who need power features—such as multi-page batch signing ("Sign All Pages"), camera paper signature scanning, and multiple company profile management—SwiftSign offers an optional Pro pass available exclusively through Google Play.

Mandatory Legal Disclaimer

SwiftSign creates graphical electronic signatures and business stamps for everyday documents. Acceptance and legal requirements for electronic signatures vary by document type and jurisdiction. SwiftSign does not provide cryptographic digital signatures, certificate-based signing, notarization or legal advice.
