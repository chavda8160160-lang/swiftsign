Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my signed documents stored?

All documents are stored exclusively on your device. SwiftSign does not upload documents to any server. When you tap Finish & Sign, the file is saved directly to your Downloads or Pictures directory.

How do I manage my Google Play subscription?

All Pro subscriptions are managed directly through your Google Play Store account under Play Store → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can cancel or switch plans at any time with no cancellation fees.

Can I unlock Pro on the website?

SwiftSign on the web is Free only. Pro features (such as the Paper Scanner and Multi-page Sign All Pages) are available exclusively in the SwiftSign Android application via Google Play Billing.
