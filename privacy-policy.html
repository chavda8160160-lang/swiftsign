Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 • Effective immediately
Zero Data Collection Promise: SwiftSign is engineered from the ground up as a 100% on-device document signing application. Your documents, signatures, images, and files never leave your device. We do not operate document servers, databases, or cloud storage for your files.

1. Introduction

SwiftSign ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates and outlines our data practices.

2. Document Processing & Local Storage

  • 100% On-Device Processing: When you open a PDF or image, place your signature, and export the signed file, all computations occur entirely within your device's memory.
  • No Server Uploads: Your documents, signatures, and biometric stroke coordinates are NEVER uploaded, transmitted, or synchronized to any external server.
  • Local Storage: Your saved signatures and weekly usage counters are stored exclusively in your device's private sandbox storage (SharedPreferences / LocalStorage). You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android settings.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

SwiftSign offers optional subscriptions and premium tiers through Google Play In-App Billing.

  • All financial transactions, credit card numbers, payment credentials, and billing details are processed securely and exclusively by Google Play.
  • SwiftSign does not collect, receive, or store your payment details, bank credentials, or credit card numbers.
  • Google Play provides us only with anonymous purchase receipts and subscription status tokens to verify your entitlement.

4. Permissions Used

SwiftSign requests only the minimal permissions required to function:

  • Storage / Media Permissions: Used solely to allow you to select documents from your device and to save the signed output to your public Downloads or Pictures folders.
  • Internet Permission: Used exclusively to communicate with Google Play Billing for in-app purchases and to check atomic network time to prevent device clock manipulation.

5. Third-Party Services & Analytics

SwiftSign does not include third-party advertising SDKs or invasive tracking trackers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data.

6. Children's Privacy

SwiftSign does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@swiftsign.in
